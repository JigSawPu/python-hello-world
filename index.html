Our story

Good coffee starts with good attention.

We roast in small batches, taste every lot, and keep our menu intentionally focused. The goal is simple: coffee with clarity, sweetness, and enough character to remember.

But a café is more than what lands in the cup. Ember & Oak was built to be a soft place in a loud day — welcoming, unhurried, and always ready with another chair.
